Synthesis thermodynamics: Cd16Se13Cl6, Cd68Se55Cl26

Dots Cd16Se13Cl6, Cd68Se55Cl26 in equilibrium with CdSe and CdCl2 monomers in solution, including every ligand-stripped state found by the desorption search. Free energies: MACE-MH-1 (harmonic vibrations, rigid-rotor rotation, ideal solutes at 1 M) plus Generalized Born solvation of the GFN2-xTB charges. For each temperature the coupled equilibria ci = exp(−ΔG°i/kBT) cCdSeni cCdCl2mi, with mass balances CCdSe = cCdSe + Σ ni ci and CCdCl2 = cCdCl2 + Σ mi ci, are solved for the free monomers (nested bisection in log space). Each panel has its own controls, only for the quantities that enter it.

Yield against temperature

Fraction of all CdSe held in each dot family (summed over its ligand-stripped states), as free monomer and, if enabled, as bulk. Dashed: the dissolution onset Tdiss, where the dots hold half of the CdSe, at these concentrations.

Populations against the [CdCl2]/[CdSe] ratio

At one temperature and total [CdSe]: how the ligand-to-monomer stoichiometry selects between the dot families and the free monomers, i.e. whether growth stops at a small, atomically precise cluster or proceeds to the larger dot.

Free monomers and supersaturation

Free [CdSe] and [CdCl2] in equilibrium, and the supersaturation S = cCdSe / csat against bulk CdSe (csat = exp[−(GsolCdSe − Gbulk)/kBT]). S > 1: supersaturated with respect to bulk growth, the driving force of nucleation and ripening.

Ligand shell against temperature

Mean number of CdCl2 units bound per dot of each family in the equilibrium population: how the shell desorbs as T rises at these concentrations.
Δμprec versus concentration: the monomer chemical potential is μ = G°(molecule) + ΔGsolv + Δμprec + kBT ln c. The concentration term is the ideal-dilution entropy of the free monomer and scales with T; Δμprec is a constant offset for a monomer that is really bound in a molecular precursor, which lies well below the bare gas-phase molecule.
Caveats: the monomers are the bare CdSe and CdCl2 molecules unless the precursor stabilisation is set; implicit solvation omits specific coordination by L-type ligands. Only the dot sizes and stripped states computed here are present, so the populations are relative to this set of species.
