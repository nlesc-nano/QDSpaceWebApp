Intensity Ik ∝ (ν0 − νk)4/νk · Sk / (1 − e−hcνk/kBT), 532 nm, 300 K, normalised to its maximum; the axis is scaled to the strongest feature above 50 cm−1 (1/ν and the Bose factor inflate the lowest modes). Dotted: depolarised part I⊥ = ρ/(1 + ρ) I, with ρ = 3γ′²/(45a′² + 4γ′²); totally symmetric modes are polarised (ρ < ¾) and nearly vanish in it. Vibrational energies

ZPE = ½ Σk ħωk; Uvib(T) = ZPE + Σk ħωk / (eħωk/kBT − 1); Fvib = Uvib − T Svib. Both start at ZPE (dotted) at T → 0; their gap is the entropic term T Svib, which lowers free energies as T rises.

Vibrational entropy and heat capacity

With xk = ħωk/kBT: Svib = kB Σk [xk/(exk − 1) − ln(1 − e−xk)] and Cv = kB Σk xk² exk / (exk − 1)². Cv saturates at the classical (3N − 6) kB = 38.00 meV/K (dotted) once kBT exceeds the highest modes; Svib keeps growing, mostly from the soft modes.

Stability & ligands in vacuum

Decomposition free energy

ΔGdec = G[(CdSe)55(CdCl2)13] − 55 G[CdSe, bulk] − 13 G[CdCl2, 1 M], per CdSe unit (solid; dotted: the electronic ΔEdec). It is the excess (surface) free energy of the dot against bulk CdSe with the ligands in solution: positive means metastable against ripening into the bulk, and it decreases as the dot grows.

Binding free energy

ΔGbind = (G[(CdSe)55(CdCl2)13] − 55 G[CdSe, 1 M] − 13 G[CdCl2, 1 M]) / 68, per unit (solid; dotted: electronic ΔEbind): the free energy of assembling the dot from monomers. More negative is more strongly bound; it rises with T because the free monomers gain translational and rotational entropy.

Stepwise CdCl2 desorption

ΔEk = E(dotk) + E(CdCl2) − E(dotk−1), where dotk is the k-th structure of the lowest path found (bars, MACE-relaxed). ΔGk(300 K) adds the vibrational change and the translational and rotational entropy of the released CdCl2 at 1 M (circles). Grey dots at k = 1: every symmetry-distinct surface site. Positive: bound. Beam search (width 2): units re-enumerated on the relaxed surface after every removal; units within 11 Å of the last removal relaxed, the others carried over and relaxed before being chosen (486 MACE relaxations).
References computed with the same MACE head: bulk CdSe (cell-relaxed, phonons) and the CdSe and CdCl2 monomers as ideal gases at a 1 M standard state (harmonic vibrations, rigid-rotor rotation; the dot is treated the same way). No solvent: everything here is in vacuum; the next section adds solvation and finite concentrations, and the equilibrium ligand shell.

Stability & ligands in solution

The vacuum free energies of the previous section plus an implicit solvent and finite concentrations: μi = G°i(T) + ΔGsolv,i(ε) + kBT ln(ci / 1 M), bulk CdSe as a solid. ΔGsolv(ε) = −½ (1 − 1/ε) Σij qiqj/fGB(rij): Generalized Born electrostatics of the GFN2-xTB charges, one single point at each MACE-MH-1 gas-phase geometry (no re-optimisation in solvent; non-electrostatic terms not included; ALPB offered for the named solvents where it converged for every species). Each panel has its own controls, only for the quantities that enter it.

Decomposition free energy in solution

ΔGdec = [Gsol(dot) − 55 G(CdSe, bulk) − 13 μ(CdCl2)] / 55, per CdSe, against T. CdSe is the bulk solid, so only the solvent and [CdCl2] enter. Positive: the dot is metastable against ripening into bulk CdSe with its ligands dissolved; more CdCl2 in solution stabilises it. Dotted: vacuum, 1 M.

Binding free energy and dissolution temperature

ΔGbind = [Gsol(dot) − 55 μ(CdSe) − 13 μ(CdCl2)] / 68, per unit, against T. The dot assembles where it is negative and dissolves into monomers above Tdiss (ΔGbind = 0), where the monomers' kBT ln c entropy overcomes the bonding; dilution lowers Tdiss. Δμprec versus concentration: the monomer chemical potential is μ = G°(molecule) + ΔGsolv + Δμprec + kBT ln c. The concentration term is the ideal-dilution entropy of the free monomer and scales with T; Δμprec is a constant offset for a monomer that is really bound in a molecular precursor, which lies well below the bare gas-phase molecule.

Stepwise CdCl2 desorption at T

ΔGk = Gsol(dotk) − Gsol(dotk−1) + μ(CdCl2) along the lowest removal path. Negative bars: that unit desorbs spontaneously at this T and [CdCl2].
