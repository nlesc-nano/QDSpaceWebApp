Raman spectrum (non-resonant)

Activity Sk = 45a′² + 7γ′² from the CP2K PBE Raman tensors ∂α/∂R projected on each mode (finite field ±0.002 a.u.), with a′ its isotropic part and γ′ its anisotropy. Intensity Ik ∝ (ν0 − νk)4/νk · Sk / (1 − e−hcνk/kBT), 532 nm, 300 K, normalised to its maximum; the axis is scaled to the strongest feature above 50 cm−1 (1/ν and the Bose factor inflate the lowest modes). Dotted: depolarised part I⊥ = ρ/(1 + ρ) I, with ρ = 3γ′²/(45a′² + 4γ′²); totally symmetric modes are polarised (ρ < ¾) and nearly vanish in it. Measured QD spectra are usually resonant (LO-enhanced): compare positions and symmetries, not relative intensities.

Vibrational energies

ZPE = ½ Σk ħωk; Uvib(T) = ZPE + Σk ħωk / (eħωk/kBT − 1); Fvib = Uvib − T Svib. Both start at ZPE (dotted) at T → 0; their gap is the entropic term T Svib, which lowers free energies as T rises.

Vibrational entropy and heat capacity

With xk = ħωk/kBT: Svib = kB Σk [xk/(exk − 1) − ln(1 − e−xk)] and Cv = kB Σk xk² exk / (exk − 1)². Cv saturates at the classical (3N − 6) kB = 38.00 meV/K (dotted) once kBT exceeds the highest modes; Svib keeps growing, mostly from the soft modes.
